# Just 3 Days Privacy Policy (v1.1)

**Effective date:** July 31, 2026
**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

## 1. Overview and controller

DreamAppLab ("we," "us," or the "Operator") operates Just 3 Days (the "Service") and processes user information under applicable privacy laws.

- **Controller/Operator:** DreamAppLab
- **Privacy contact:** [demianjun1@gmail.com](mailto:demianjun1@gmail.com)

## 2. Information processed and purposes

| Category | Information | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| On-device goals and records | Goal title, difficulty, start date, duration, reminder time, daily completion records, restart history | Goal management, progress, statistics, reminders, and restart features |
| On-device settings | Language, theme, notification and display settings, four-digit PIN, ad-removal status | Preferences, local app lock, and purchase-status display |
| Sign in with Apple | Apple user identifier and public or private relay email | Sign-in, identity verification, and account connection |
| Google Sign-In | Google account identifier, email, name, and profile-image URL when provided | Sign-in, identity verification, and account connection |
| Firebase Authentication | Firebase UID, authentication provider, and authentication status | Authentication and account management |
| Cloud Firestore | Synchronized goals, daily records, restart information, ad-removal status and purchase date, update and deletion metadata | Multi-device synchronization and restoration |
| In-app purchase | Product and transaction identifiers, purchase date, verified entitlement | Providing, verifying, and restoring ad removal |
| Advertising and consent | IP address, device, OS and app information, available advertising-related identifiers, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostics, consent status | Ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, consent management, and troubleshooting |
| Firebase App Check | App-attestation token and technical app/device information | Preventing unauthorized backend access |

Without an account connection, goals and records normally stay on the device and are not synchronized to Cloud Firestore. We do not directly collect payment-card details or Face ID facial data. iOS performs Face ID matching and returns only the authentication result to the Service.

## 3. How information is collected and legal bases

Information is entered or generated when you create a goal, record progress, change settings, sign in, purchase, or make a consent choice. The app and its SDKs may automatically transmit technical information when they operate.

Depending on applicable law, we rely on performance of the service you request for core features, account synchronization and purchases; consent for optional advertising where required; legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and troubleshooting; and compliance with legal obligations. Refusing an optional account connection or notifications limits synchronization or reminders but does not prevent on-device goal management.

## 4. Uses

- Provide goals, records, progress, statistics, regular reminders, and one restart reminder after seven days without practice
- Provide Apple/Google sign-in, account management, and multi-device synchronization
- Verify, provide, and restore the ad-removal purchase
- Deliver and measure Google AdMob ads and prevent fraud
- Manage advertising consent and privacy choices
- Maintain security, stability and quality, and respond to requests

## 5. Advertising and tracking

The Service may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads and Google User Messaging Platform to obtain consent and provide privacy choices where required. This version does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use IDFA for cross-app tracking. Non-personalized or limited ads may be shown based on consent, region, and device settings.

## 7. Storage and international processing

Synchronized Cloud Firestore data is stored in the named database `just3db` in Google Cloud's Seoul region (`asia-northeast3`). Firebase Authentication, App Check, Google Sign-In, AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Sign in with Apple, and App Store processing may occur in countries outside yours where Google or Apple operates. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 8. Retention and deletion

| Location/information | Retention and deletion |
|---|---|
| On-device goals and records | Until you delete the relevant data or app data |
| UserDefaults settings | Until you delete the setting or app data |
| Keychain PIN and session data | Until the feature is disabled, the item is explicitly deleted, or iOS removes it; some items may survive reinstallation |
| Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore | Until account deletion, then deleted without undue delay unless law requires retention |
| App Store purchase and transaction records | Under Apple's policies and applicable law |
| Advertising and diagnostic information | Under Google's policies and applicable law |

Deleting your account in the app deletes the Firebase Authentication account and associated Cloud Firestore data. Signing out does not delete server data. Account deletion does not automatically delete goals and records stored on that device, and Apple controls App Store purchase records.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, or an end to third-party disclosure.

- Delete an account in "Account Management."
- Delete goals and records from their in-app screens.
- Change notification permission in iOS Settings.
- Change advertising choices from "Ad privacy settings" when available.
- Send other requests, with the registered email and requested action, to [demianjun1@gmail.com](mailto:demianjun1@gmail.com).

We may verify identity before responding. We normally respond electronically without charge unless applicable law permits otherwise. EEA and UK users may also complain to their local data-protection authority.

## 10. Security and children

We use TLS, user-scoped Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules, Firebase App Check, Keychain storage, least-privilege access, and SDK/security updates. We respond to incidents as required by law. The Service is not intended to create a linked account for a child without any consent required from a parent or guardian.
